Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version
  • Reel Words works without an account. If you don’t sign in, your entries never leave your device.
  • If you sign in, we store your email address and your entries so they can sync between your devices.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. We don’t sell or share your data.
  • You can export everything or permanently delete your account at any time from Settings.

Who we are

Reel Words is an independent, open-source project for saving and reviewing English words, phrases and lines from movies and TV shows. The source code is public on GitHub, so you can see exactly how your data is handled.

Using Reel Words without an account

Everything you add is stored only in your browser on your device, using IndexedDB and localStorage. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to any server. If you clear your browser’s site data, uninstall the browser or use private browsing, those entries may be lost. Use Settings → Export to keep a backup.

What we collect when you sign in

DataWhy
Your email addressTo create your account and send you sign-in links. We don’t send marketing email.
Your entries (words, meanings, notes, tags, movie titles, timestamps, review progress)To back them up and sync them across your devices.
Basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and sign-in times)Recorded automatically by our infrastructure providers for security and abuse prevention.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, location or any payment information.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of third-party services to run Reel Words:

  • Supabase stores your account and synced entries and handles sign-in. Database rules make sure each account can only read and write its own entries.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails, so it receives your email address.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, it may log visitors’ IP addresses for security.
  • jsDelivr serves the sync library from a public CDN when you use cloud sync.
  • Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) looks up definitions. It receives only the word you look up, and only when you press Look up.

Links to Cambridge, Merriam-Webster and YouGlish open those websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Cookies and local storage

Reel Words uses no advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage only to make the app work: your entries, your preferences (such as theme and voice) and, if you sign in, your sign-in session.

How long we keep your data

Synced data is kept for as long as you have an account. When you delete an entry, it is marked as deleted so the deletion can sync to your other devices. When you delete your account, it and all its entries are permanently removed from our database right away. Copies may remain in our providers’ routine backups for a short period before those backups expire.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: go to Settings → Export to download all your entries as JSON or CSV.
  • Correct: edit any entry directly in the app.
  • Delete: delete individual entries, or go to Settings → Delete account to erase your account and all synced data.
  • Stop syncing: sign out at any time and keep using the app locally.

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or Singapore’s PDPA), you may have additional rights. Contact us and we’ll help.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and access to synced data is restricted per account at the database level. No system is perfectly secure, though, so please don’t store sensitive personal information in your entries.

Children

Reel Words is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests? Open an issue on GitHub.

隐私政策

简短版本
  • Reel Words 不注册也能使用。不登录的话，你的记录永远不会离开你的设备。
  • 登录后，我们会保存你的邮箱地址和你的记录，以便在多台设备间同步。
  • 没有广告、没有统计分析、没有追踪。我们不会出售或分享你的数据。
  • 你可以随时在设置中导出全部数据，或永久注销账号。

关于我们

Reel Words 是一个独立的开源项目，用于记录和复习英文电影、剧集中的单词、短语和句子。源代码公开在 GitHub 上，你可以直接查看我们是如何处理数据的。

不登录时

你添加的所有内容只保存在你设备上的浏览器里（IndexedDB 和 localStorage）。我们看不到这些数据，它们也不会被发送到任何服务器。如果你清除浏览器的网站数据、卸载浏览器或使用无痕模式，这些记录可能会丢失。请通过设置 → 导出备份保留一份副本。

登录后我们收集的信息

数据用途
你的邮箱地址创建账号并发送登录链接。我们不会发送营销邮件。
你的记录（单词、释义、笔记、标签、影视名称、时间点、复习进度）备份记录，并在你的多台设备之间同步。
基础技术日志（如 IP 地址、浏览器类型、登录时间）由基础设施服务商自动记录，用于安全防护和防止滥用。

我们不会索取你的姓名、电话、位置或任何支付信息。

第三方服务

Reel Words 依赖以下少数第三方服务运行：

  • Supabase：存储你的账号和同步的记录，并负责登录。数据库规则确保每个账号只能读写自己的记录。
  • Resend：发送登录邮件，因此会接收到你的邮箱地址。
  • GitHub Pages：托管本网站。和其他网站托管服务一样，它可能出于安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址。
  • jsDelivr：使用云同步时，从公共 CDN 加载同步组件。
  • Free Dictionary API（dictionaryapi.dev）：查询英文释义。只有在你点击查词时，它才会收到你查询的那个单词。

有道、剑桥、Merriam-Webster、YouGlish 等外部链接会打开对应的网站，它们有各自的隐私政策。

Cookie 与本地存储

Reel Words 不使用任何广告或追踪 Cookie。浏览器本地存储仅用于让应用正常运行：保存你的记录、偏好设置（如主题、语音、语言），以及登录后的登录状态。

数据保留期限

账号存在期间，同步的数据会一直保留。删除一条记录时，它会被标记为“已删除”，以便这个删除操作同步到你的其他设备。注销账号时，账号及其所有记录会立即从数据库中永久删除；服务商的例行备份中可能会短暂保留副本，直到这些备份过期。

你的选择与权利

  • 查阅与导出：在设置 → 导出备份中下载全部记录（JSON 或 CSV）。
  • 更正：直接在应用中编辑任意记录。
  • 删除：删除单条记录，或在设置 → 注销账号中删除账号及所有云端数据。
  • 停止同步：随时退出登录，继续在本地使用。

根据你所在地区的法律（例如欧盟、英国的 GDPR，新加坡的 PDPA，或中国的《个人信息保护法》），你可能享有其他权利。如有需要，请联系我们。

安全

所有通信均通过 HTTPS 加密，云端数据在数据库层面按账号隔离访问。但没有任何系统是绝对安全的，请不要在记录中保存敏感的个人信息。

儿童

Reel Words 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童，我们也不会有意收集他们的个人信息。如果你认为有儿童注册了账号，请联系我们，我们会将其删除。

政策变更

如果本政策有所修改，我们会更新页面顶部的日期；重大变更也会在应用内告知。
